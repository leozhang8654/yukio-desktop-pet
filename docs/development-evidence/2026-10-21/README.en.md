This archive preserves 9,192 development files: Qianxia concepts, segmentation and animation iterations, existing independent-agent reviews and failures, Yukio interaction experiments, answer-channel diagnostics, local activity-collector source, and requirements/research documents. All 43 highlighted items from the local audit are included. 1,662 text, selected image and research files are also browsable under `files/`.

The files are historical evidence and a local source snapshot, not a new application release. Published app version 0.4.6, runtime code, installers and updater feeds are unchanged. Local throw and activity-observation experiments are not represented as shipped features. Historical test results are not newly executed tests, and pending native Windows or continuous visual checks remain pending.

Personal home paths, private network addresses and diagnostic identifiers are replaced in public copies. `manifest.json` records both original and public SHA-256 hashes. Cache files, installed-app backups, published/superseded installers and compiled executables are listed in `excluded.json`; source, artwork, parameters and diagnostic records are retained. Archive member hashes were verified after creation. Counts include repeated iteration copies and are not measures of human effort or authorship.

Extract all six ZIP files into the same directory to restore the `project/` and `planning/` layout. Online copies are a browseable subset; download the archives for linked images and larger resources. Verify downloads with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
